Connect YouTube through Zernio

  1. Create a free Zernio account. Use its current free plan.
  2. In Zernio, connect the YouTube channel you want to use.
  3. Create a Read & Write API key, restricted to your Watch Out profile where available.
  4. Enter the key below, then choose the channel. This does not publish anything.

The current free plan includes 2 accounts and unlimited monthly posts. YouTube has a 50-post daily cap in Zernio; your channel can have additional limits. Pricing · Limits
